# Bias test, fixed before running (24 Nov 2026)

Question: does any directional term beat a zero-mean industry polling error? Measured on 2011–2025,
the industry miss is real (about 1.4 pp per party per election) but its direction does not carry
over (consecutive-election correlation 0.08, same sign 48% of the time); the most regular pattern is a
late shift toward AD (six of six elections), which the static bias form seems to absorb at 28–56 days.

## Candidates (priors fixed now)

| | fit | what it adds to rw_nobias |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | `rw_nobias` | nothing (the accepted nowcast's form) |
| M1 | `rw_persistent08` | a persistent mean bias per party, ZeroSumNormal(0.08), plus a per-cycle miss around it (HalfNormal(0.10) scale) |
| M2 | `rw_nobias_momentum` | polls misread a party by beta x (opinion now − opinion at the last result), centred over parties; beta ~ Normal(0, 0.2), one coefficient for all parties |
| M3 | `rw_nobias_lateswing` | M0's draws shifted by the late swing of the other elections: mean of log(median at 7 days) − log(median at h) in M0's own forecasts, per party; zero at 7 days |

M3 is derived from saved M0 draws (`--late-swing`), so it costs no fits.

## Rules (`BIAS_TEST_RULES` in `src/evaluation/backtest.py`)

Unit: the election (2011, 2015, 2019, 2022, 2024, 2025). Metric: national CRPS at each model's
coverage-rule tau, chosen leave one election out, pooled over 7, 28 and 56 days. A candidate replaces
M0 only if all hold:

1. pooled national CRPS at least 5% lower than M0's;
2. lower per-election pooled CRPS in at least 5 of 6 elections;
3. still lower on average after leaving out any one election;
4. lower or equal mean CRPS at every horizon;
5. pooled seat RPS no higher than M0's;
6. exact two-sided sign-flip test over the six elections, p ≤ 0.10 (the smallest p six elections allow is 0.031).

Fits must pass the usual gate (no divergences, r-hat ≤ 1.01, ESS ≥ 400). Otherwise M0 stays. Whatever
the verdict, freeze all four forecasts for the next election as the out-of-sample check.

## Run

```bash
OUT=outputs/backtest/WIP_grid_biastest   # with rw_nobias's arch-grid files copied in
python -m src.evaluation.backtest --models rw_persistent08 rw_nobias_momentum \
  --elections 2011-06-05 2015-10-04 2019-10-06 2022-01-30 2024-03-10 2025-05-18 \
  --horizons 56 28 7 --draws 1000 --tune 1000 --chains 4 --out $OUT
python -m src.evaluation.backtest --late-swing $OUT
python -m src.evaluation.backtest --calibrate $OUT --models rw_nobias rw_persistent08 rw_nobias_momentum rw_nobias_lateswing --horizons 7 28 56
python -m src.evaluation.backtest --bias-verdict $OUT
```
